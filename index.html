001 / THE PLACE

A calm corner
of Ajmer Road.

Tucked into Ganga Vihar in Bhakrota, Café Solace is the kind of place regulars call apna. Warm tan chairs, soft lighting under a carved wooden ceiling, a stone feature wall — a room designed for long conversations and slow afternoons.

Guests keep returning for the cozy, peaceful atmosphere and service that feels warm and attentive. People spend anywhere from thirty minutes to four unhurried hours here.
